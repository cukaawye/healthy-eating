High Protein Buffalo Ranch Pasta Salad

Jump to Recipe

This Buffalo Ranch Pasta Salad feels like buffalo wings crashed a backyard cookout and decided to stay. It’s bold, a touch spicy, creamy where it counts, and somehow still feels light enough for a hot day.

I’m obsessed with buffalo sauce! My first Whole30 back in 2017 sparked that love, so when I call this pasta salad a top pick for game‑day snacks, barbecues, or even a random Tuesday, I truly mean it. It captures everything I adore about buffalo wings without the sticky fingers. It can serve as an appetizer, side, or a full meal depending on how you pile it on. I love pairing it with my buffalo turkey meatballs, and it also goes great with fried shrimp for a quick weeknight dinner.

Every time I whip this up, it vanishes in minutes, and it’s easy to see why. Join me and discover how straightforward it is to create this crowd‑pleaser!

What You'll Need

Possible Swaps

Pasta- The protein boost in this dish comes from chickpea pasta. You can use any brand or shape of chickpea pasta.

Ranch- I opt for Primal Kitchen’s ranch to make this dish dairy‑free, though any ranch will do.

Buffalo sauce- I love using Franks as my go‑to hot sauce, though any brand or spice level of buffalo sauce works for you.

How can I add even more protein?

Looking to make it a complete meal? Stir in shredded rotisserie chicken or grilled chicken pieces. If you prefer no meat, add white beans or chickpeas for a satisfying, hearty bite.

How do I keep chickpea pasta from getting mushy?

Chickpea pasta can be a bit finicky, yet it’s completely manageable. Cook it slightly less than the recommended time—typically 6-8 minutes works well—and rinse it in cold water to halt the cooking process. This way it stays nicely al dente.

Step‑by‑Step Instructions

  1. Cook pasta until al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
  2. While that's cooking, dice up your celery, bell pepper, and green onion.
  3. Whisk together ranch, buffalo sauce, dill, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth. Taste. Want more heat? Add it.
  4. In a big bowl, combine the pasta and veggies, then pour the dressing on and toss until everything is coated.
  5. Chill at least 30 minutes before serving.

Helpful Hints

  • Save some dressing. The pasta loves to drink it all up while it sits. Stir in a little extra before serving and it'll taste fresh again.
  • Watch your heat. Buffalo sauces aren't all made equal some are mild, some light your lips on fire. Start small and adjust.
  • Don't skip the chill. Even 30 minutes in the fridge makes a huge difference. If you've got the patience, longer is even better.
  • Add some cheese. Adding blue cheese crumbles or fresh mozzarella in this would be so good!

Storage and Leftovers

Place any leftovers into a sealed container and keep them in the refrigerator for 3-4 days. As the pasta sits, it absorbs the dressing, so give it a boost with another spoonful of ranch or a splash of buffalo sauce before you dive in.

Buffalo Ranch Protein Pasta Salad

This Buffalo Ranch Protein Pasta Salad is creamy, spicy, and loaded with fresh crunch. A gluten free, high protein side dish that's perfect for meal prep or parties.

PrepPrep Time 10 mins
CookCook Time 8 mins
TotalTotal Time 18 mins
Servings8 people

Ingredients

  • ▢1 package chickpea protein pasta
  • ▢1 stalk celery diced
  • ▢½ red bell pepper diced
  • ▢1 green onion diced

Dressing

  • ▢⅓ cup ranch *I use Primal Kitchen's Ranch to keep this dairy free
  • ▢3 tablespoons buffalo sauce *I use Frank's Original Hot Sauce
  • ▢½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ▢½ teaspoon dried dill
  • ▢¼ teaspoon salt
  • ▢¼ teaspoon pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta until al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
  2. While that's cooking, dice up your celery, bell pepper, and green onion.
  3. Whisk together ranch, buffalo sauce, dill, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth. Taste. Want more heat? Add some more buffalo sauce.
  4. In a big bowl , combine the pasta and veggies, then pour the dressing on and toss until everything is coated.
  5. Chill at least 30 minutes before serving.
