1,800 Calorie Gluten-Free Meal Plan

A thoughtfully designed 1,800 calorie gluten‑free eating plan can help people who want to lose weight develop healthier habits. Remember that individual calorie requirements differ, so your intake should match your personal objectives and daily routine.

Who should try this diet?

If you’re concentrating on losing weight, aiming for roughly 1,800 calories each day can be a very effective strategy. This amount supplies enough fuel for everyday tasks and workouts while still creating a calorie shortfall that supports weight loss.

Naturally, only people with a moderate level of activity should follow this calorie level.

Morning Meal

Coco Loco Nutty Blend

For the best texture, use a banana that’s been chilled or even frozen in this delicious nut‑filled blend.

Calorie Count and Macros

505 Calories 31g Protein 61g Carbohydrates 18g Fat

Required Ingredients

1 medium [Banana] 40 grams [Oats] 40 ml Coconut Milk Drink (carton) 260 ml [Almond Milk] 20 grams [Peanut Butter (smooth)] 20 grams [Vanilla Whey Protein (80%)]

Preparation Steps

Step 1: Put the oats and milk into your favourite blender and let them sit to soften for about 1 minute before you add the remaining ingredients.

Step 2: Secure the lid tightly and blend for roughly 15 – 30 seconds, adding water gradually if needed to reach the consistency you prefer.

Step 3: Pour the mixture into a glass and enjoy a smoothie that’ll make you go a little nuts!

Midday Meal

Greek‑Style Chicken Stack!

Unleash that Olympic strength!

Calorie Count and Macros

Calories: 517 Protein: 35g Carbohydrates: 53g Fat: 17g

Required Ingredients

You’ll need 100 grams [Chicken Breast], 4 slices [Gluten-free Multiseed Bread], 1/2 medium [Red Onion], 4 pieces [Sun-Dried Tomatoes], a pinch of Black Pepper, 40 grams [Greek Yogurt (whole milk)], 2 leaves of [Iceberg Lettuce], a pinch of Salt, and a pinch of Dried Oregano.

Preparation Steps

Step 1: Pre‑grill the chicken, then let it cool in the fridge before you start assembling the sandwich.

Step 2: Thinly slice the red onion and toss it in a bowl with the Greek yogurt, salt, pepper, and oregano, then stir until well combined.

Step 3: Lightly toast the whole‑grain bread to your desired level of crispness… (toasting helps prevent a soggy sandwich!).

Step 4: Spread the yogurt mixture evenly on both slices of bread, then layer in this order: bottom slice, chicken, lettuce, sliced sun‑dried tomato, top slice.

Step 5: Dig in and enjoy!

Evening Meal

Easy Chicken, Broccoli & Rice Meal Prep!

Calorie Count and Macros

Calories: 500 Protein: 31g Carbohydrates: 67g Fat: 12g

Required Ingredients

Gather 80 grams [Brown Rice], 100 grams [Chicken Breast], 1/4 tsp [Brown Sugar], 1/4 tsp Paprika, 1/4 tsp Cumin Powder, 1/4 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 1/2 tsp [Olive Oil], and 70 grams [Broccoli].

Preparation Steps

Step 1: Begin by placing the rice in a saucepan, using a 1‑to‑2 ratio of rice to water, and set it over medium heat.

Step 2: Let it simmer gently for about 15‑20 minutes until the water is fully absorbed.

Step 3: Meanwhile, combine the chicken breast with brown sugar, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a bowl.

Step 4: Heat your favorite frying pan or skillet over a medium‑high flame.

Step 5: Pour in the olive oil, add the chicken, and cook it roughly 5 minutes on each side.

Step 6: When the chicken is nicely browned on both sides, switch off the heat and let it rest for another 5 minutes.

Step 7: While the chicken rests, bring a pot of water to a rapid boil and drop in the broccoli florets, blanching them for 1 minute.

Step 8: Cut the chicken into bite‑sized pieces, then (if you’re meal‑prepping) pull out your containers and split the portions evenly among them.

Step 9: For meal prep, seal the containers and store in the fridge for up to 4 days. When ready to eat, microwave on high for 2 minutes (a dash of spicy sauce makes it extra tasty!).

You’re all set!
