13 Self-Care Sunday Ideas for a More Refreshed Week | Self-care ideas | Self-care printable

Everyone could use an ultimate self‑care Sunday, especially since weekdays often become hectic with work, household chores, and more.

Sadly, many of us experience the Sunday blues.

When I was still working full‑time, Sundays could be challenging; the idea of rising early and facing another stressful week took the joy out of them.

That’s why I put together this Ultimate Self‑Care Sunday list to help you start the week feeling refreshed.

Why are self‑care Sundays important?

First, we should understand what self‑care truly means.

Self‑care is fairly self‑explanatory: it involves doing things that nourish your mind, body, and soul.

After a hectic week, taking time to unwind is essential, and you’ll surely enjoy the rewards.

A nationwide U.S. survey demonstrated that self‑care produced the following effects:

  • Boosted self‑confidence for 64% of participants
  • Raised productivity levels in 67% of respondents
  • Elevated happiness for 71% of those surveyed

Explore the self‑care ideas below and begin crafting your own routine. Staying flexible is key, or the routine can feel like a chore.

13 Self‑Care Sunday Ideas for an Amazing Week

  1. Prioritize restorative sleep

Don’t worry about sleeping an extra hour or two. Sleeping in on weekends isn’t lazy and isn’t a scandal—unless you’re like my grandmother, who would never be found in bed after 7 am, bless her heart.

That’s easy for me, because I’m a full‑time night owl!

Weekends shouldn’t feel like another regular workday. I suggest waking up gradually to set the tone for the rest of your day.

Making a mental shift on weekends is crucial; you don’t want to be tangled up in a work project due next week.

I recommend waking up slowly and spending a few minutes in bed gazing out the window.

That’s the perfect way to begin your self‑care Sunday.

  1. Savor your favorite self‑care Sunday drink

I have to admit, this is my daily morning highlight!

I cannot wait to put on the kettle and make myself a cup of brew. The smell of coffee in the morning just tickles my brain.

Rest assured, I know caffeine first thing in the morning is unhealthy.

But I am going to do it anyway. The beauty of freedom of choice coming to fruition.

If you are looking for a healthier option, I got you covered:

  • Smoothies – This is a great source of energy and vitamins, not to mention delicious! Smoothies can also keep you full which in return makes it a great option for weight loss. And there are many more benefits.
  • Matcha tea – This is a form of green tea and is very healthy. It is high in antioxidants and boosts brain power etc.
  • Water – I know this sounds extremely boring but you can always spice it up by adding a lemon or drinking unsweetened sparkling water.

  1. Get some Jesus time

Sunday mornings are crucial for quiet time with God.

If you start your week spiritually sated, I can guarantee your week will be smoother.

Take some time in the morning to attend church or watch an online sermon. You can also do Bible study and spend time in prayer.

  1. No makeup Sunday

This one goes hand in hand with the cozy clothes.

Some days I love a full glam makeup look and other days I keep it bare. (Hi, it’s 2024 Steph, I barely ever wear make-up these days).

If you are someone that has to wear makeup every day to work, I will advise you to have one day in your week be the no makeup day. This can be a great way to give your skin a break and allow it to breathe.

And there are some benefits as well:

  • Smaller pores
  • Less oiliness
  • Less acne
  • A deeper sense of self-confidence

Not to mention you get 20-30 minutes of your day extra that you would not have.

  1. Social media detox

I cannot stress the importance of this enough. I really believe that social media is the reason for a lot of our problems in society today.

Social media is toxic and often leads to the annoying doom scroll.

Just try and build a habit where you do not open Instagram or TikTok every time you get bored.

The goal should be to go one entire day or more without social media.

Besides the benefits heavily outweigh the cons:

  • More time for meaningful in-person interactions
  • Less anxiety and FOMO
  • Fewer opportunities to compare yourself which means,
  • Higher confidence over time
  • More time to be productive
  • More time to learn hobbies – Here are 40+ hobbies to try today!

  1. Enjoy a hearty meal

It has been made so easy for our generation to opt for fast food. It is one call away or it can even be delivered to our doors.

But nothing beats a homemade hearty dish. Some cooked chicken with rice and vegetable sides. Mouthwatering.

It is so much more beneficial to opt for healthier homemade food.

And it does not have to be so difficult. There are options such as Hello Fresh etc. All your ingredients come prepacked to your home and it is customed to your needs.

Make a point to cook on Sundays.

  1. Go outside

Take a 10-minute walk outside. Just go feel some grass under your feet.

I am my best self once I am surrounded by nature and fresh air.

If you do not feel like walking, grab a glass of juice and go sit outside. Listen to the birds singing, look at the clouds, and feel the sun on your skin. It does not get better than that.

My number one cure when I felt down was to always plan a day trip to my nearest nature reserve. I call it my therapy. There is nothing more soothing than drinking a coffee in the cold early mornings at a dam and looking at the antelopes enjoying daybreak.

Trust me, going outside is the best medicine for mental health. It is where I feel happiest. You cannot miss out on incorporating this into your self-care Sunday routine!

  1. Journal on self-care Sunday

Journaling can be a good tool for our minds. Sometimes it makes it easier to lay everything out on paper.

If you struggle with journaling, you can always use prompts and it is so easy to find on Pinterest.

Otherwise, you can just brain-dump, that is what I do.

Another way is to just write 3-5 things down that you are grateful for. Sometimes you can get so overwhelmed with life that you forget there are still things you can be grateful for.

I also use the book “A 5 Year Question a Day Memory Journal”. Every day there is a new question and you repeat that question every year on the same day. I am really curious to see my life unfold in front of me and my character development.

  1. Watch your favourite show

What is a self-care Sunday without a lazy series binge?

Take your day, kick back, and relax while binging a new or your favorite show.

Once again there is nothing wrong with it. I used to feel ashamed for binging series, Heaven knows why? In the end, why should anyone care what you do with your day?

Check out the top Netflix series and pick one. For me, it is currently Queen Charlotte a Bridgerton Story. Or if I want to go for a comfort show, Vampire Diaries.
